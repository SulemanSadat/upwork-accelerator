# Privacy Policy — Upwork Accelerator

**Last updated:** September 22, 2026

Upwork Accelerator is an independent third-party Chrome extension developed by **Suleman Sadat**. This Privacy Policy explains how the extension handles information when you use it.

## 1. Information We Collect

Upwork Accelerator does **not intentionally collect, sell, or transmit personally identifiable information**.

The extension does not collect or store:

* Names, email addresses, or contact information
* Passwords or authentication credentials
* Payment or financial information
* Private messages or communications
* Location data
* Browsing history outside the extension's intended functionality

## 2. Website Information

The extension may read relevant information that is already available on Upwork pages while you are using Upwork.

This may include information associated with Upwork job listings and client information, such as Hire Rate, when available.

This information is used solely to provide the extension's in-feed job insights and functionality.

## 3. Local Storage

Upwork Accelerator may use Chrome's local storage functionality to save extension settings and temporarily cache relevant information during your browsing session.

This is used to improve performance and reduce unnecessary processing or repeated requests.

Stored information is not intentionally transmitted to third parties by the extension.

## 4. Data Sharing

Upwork Accelerator does not sell, rent, or transfer user data to third parties.

The extension does not use user data for advertising, creditworthiness, lending decisions, or purposes unrelated to its single purpose.

## 5. Remote Code

The extension does not use remote code. Its executable code is included within the extension package.

## 6. Security

The extension is designed to minimize unnecessary data access and processing. However, no software can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Upwork Accelerator is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Upwork.

The extension does not intentionally transmit collected information to third-party services.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
